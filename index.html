About the Club

Our Mission

The FMP club is dedicated to empowering and preparing high school students who aspire to enter the healthcare field. We provide an interactive environment to explore diverse medical careers, develop practical skills, and build a supportive community.

Who We Are

Founded by students, for students, FMP is a club at Tracy High for anyone curious about medicine. Everyone is welcome, no experience needed.

Why Join FMP?

Explore Career Paths

Discover the specialties before college and find the one that fits: nursing, surgery, pediatrics, emergency medicine, radiology, pharmacy, dentistry, physical therapy, research, and public health.

Build Your Resume

Leadership experience, practical skills, certifications, and volunteer hours.

Find Your Community

Peers who share your passion for science and helping people.

Learning by Doing

Skill Workshops

Practice real clinical basics: taking vital signs, suturing, and first aid.

Medical Mysteries

Work through real case studies, symptom puzzles, lab results, and patient scenarios as a team. Some run like an escape room, all of them build clinical reasoning.

Certifications

Work toward CPR, Basic Life Support, and Stop the Bleed. These are credentials you keep.

Voices From the Field

Frontline Speaker Series

Doctors, surgeons, nurses, paramedics, and physical therapists come in to talk about what the job is actually like, the good days and the hard ones.

The Future of Medicine

Dig into artificial intelligence in medicine, bioengineering, and the technology reshaping how care gets delivered.

Medical Mixers

Trivia and Game Nights

Medical Jeopardy, Hollywood vs. Real Life, and Operation tournaments.

Medical Scavenger Hunts

Follow a trail of symptoms and medical clues hidden around the room to solve a diagnosis.

Team Socials

Fun mixers, parties, and casual hangouts to build a supportive community.

Community and Research

Research Led by Students

Run public health and medical research projects, then publish them on the FMP website as a portfolio you can point to.

Health Drives and Campaigns

Organize service initiatives and awareness campaigns around Tracy.

Red Cross Blood Drives

Partner with the American Red Cross to host and staff school and community drives.

Mentorship and Next Steps

Meet Professionals

Network with local healthcare workers who can answer the questions Google cannot.

Internships and Shadowing

Get help finding and applying to summer programs, shadowing, and hospital volunteering.

Peer Study Groups

Study sessions for IB Biology, IB Chemistry, and the AP sciences.
