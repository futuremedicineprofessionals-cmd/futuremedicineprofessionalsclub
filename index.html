About the Club

Future Medicine Professionals Club is here for any student who's ever wondered what it's like to work in healthcare — whether that means being a surgeon, a paramedic, a researcher, or something we haven't heard of yet. We meet once a month to learn from guest speakers, explore different career paths, and support each other through the confusing process of figuring out what's next.

Guest speakers

We invite people who actually work in healthcare to tell us what their job is really like — no textbook required.

Shadowing & volunteering

We share opportunities to shadow, volunteer, and get real (supervised) experience in a clinical setting.

Peer support

Upperclassmen share what actually helped them — classes to take, how to ask for a shadowing spot, how to not panic about any of it.
